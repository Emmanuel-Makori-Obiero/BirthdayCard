# Birthday Site Outcomes

- [ ] **Cinematic private-share opening** — The site opens as a polished private-share birthday experience that keeps the birthday content concealed until the recipient actively begins the surprise; the opening sequence builds anticipation before the birthday message begins.
- [ ] **Photo journey** — The supplied birthday photos are securely stored and shown in a curated film-inspired journey with dramatic framing, transitions, pacing, captions, and responsive presentation.
- [ ] **Interactive 3D avatars** — The site includes interactive stylized 3D avatars that visitors can explore or engage with as part of the birthday experience, with graceful fallback behavior if WebGL is unavailable.
- [ ] **Surprise reveal** — A deliberate interaction unveils the main birthday greeting at an emotional peak, with layered animation, celebratory effects, and an understandable call to action.
- [ ] **Responsive cinematic presentation** — The experience is responsive and polished on phones, tablets, and desktop screens, with touch-friendly controls, reduced-motion support, and accessible labels.
- [ ] **Celebratory finale and replay** — The experience ends with a celebratory finale and a clear replay option so the recipient can experience the card again.
